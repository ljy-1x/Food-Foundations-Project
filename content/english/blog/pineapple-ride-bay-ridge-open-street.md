On August 22, 2026, Food Foundations joined Pineapple Ride at the Saturday Open Street on Bay Ridge Avenue in Brooklyn. Near the ferry landing, we talked with the organizers about how a neighborhood group can get involved in its local parks. Our team also took turns arm wrestling at an outdoor table.

## From Bensonhurst Park to Bay Ridge

Earlier that month, we had joined Pineapple Ride at Bensonhurst Park, where we used food and drink examples for a walk-up nutrition activity. On August 22, we headed to a different spot along the waterfront. The Open Street was at 1 Bay Ridge Avenue, where people get off the ferry or walk in from the greenway. It was the first of six Saturdays the organizers had planned for that location.

## A conversation about caring for a park

We left Bay Ridge with a question about our own work. How could a group our size help look after a public space and use it for community activities? Pineapple Ride had an example close to home. Its members had been getting together at Bensonhurst Park for years to work on bikes and go on rides. They also helped care for the park itself.

After the event, Pineapple Ride sent us information about NYC Parks’ It’s My Park program. The organizers described jobs a group could take on, like picking up litter, spreading mulch or painting. They suggested putting out information about Food Foundations while doing the work. For us, the first question was how often we could return to the same place and take responsibility for it.

## Making a table easier to find

Someone stepping off the ferry has only a moment to decide whether to stop at a table. The organizers suggested a banner people could read from a few steps away and a single shirt color to help visitors recognize our team. Both ideas addressed a practical question we had brought with us from Bensonhurst Park. How would someone know what Food Foundations does before coming over to ask?

Thank you to Pineapple Ride for welcoming us back, making room for us at Bay Ridge and sharing what they had learned at Bensonhurst Park.
