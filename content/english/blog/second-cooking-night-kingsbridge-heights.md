On October 9, 2026, Food Foundations went back to Kingsbridge Heights Community Center in the Bronx for a second cooking workshop with the tween group. After our first visit, the center had asked the kids what meals they wanted to cook. Chicken sandwiches were on their list, so we chose them for the evening’s activity.

The first time we cooked together, the group made black bean and vegetable quesadillas. For this visit, the kids’ own suggestions helped us decide what to put on the menu.
