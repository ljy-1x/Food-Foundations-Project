On September 25, 2026, tweens at Kingsbridge Heights Community Center in the Bronx made black bean and vegetable quesadillas with Food Foundations. It was our first time leading a cooking workshop at the center.

We talked about what goes into a quesadilla while the groups cooked. The tortilla gave us a way to discuss carbohydrates, and the beans added both protein and fiber. Food Foundations led the activity, and center staff helped supervise the kitchen.

After the workshop, the center asked the kids what they wanted to make next. Their ideas included empanadas, chicken sandwiches and a Dominican breakfast called los tres golpes.
