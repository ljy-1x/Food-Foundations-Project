As the Social Media Manager for The Food Foundations Project, I create educational content that makes nutrition and public health easy to understand. I’m passionate about health and fitness, and outside of TFFP I play competitive soccer, train in MMA, and enjoy learning new things, helping others, and spending time with friends. My passion for nutrition is personal—I developed iron-deficiency anemia from not eating enough to support my activity level, which left me feeling weak and tired. Through TFFP, I learned how to improve my own health, and now I’m passionate about helping others make informed choices about nutrition and healthy living.
